## Article overview

This article explains why documentation is a practical Product Management skill rather than simply paperwork. Drawing on internship experiences at Northwestern Mutual and Veolia, it discusses writing user stories, gathering requirements, understanding the needs behind feature requests, documenting decisions, and helping teams turn stakeholder needs into actionable work. It also uses a Draymond Green / Golden State Warriors analogy to illustrate how valuable contributions often happen outside the spotlight.
